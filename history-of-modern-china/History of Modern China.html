In the 1850s-60s, as the Qing dynasty grew corrupt and weak, the 'Heavenly Kingdom' Rebellion erupted. Their leader claimed to be Jesus's younger brother.

In the late 1890s, the 'Boxer Rebellion' erupted in China. Followers believed they were invulnerable to knives and bullets.

The Empress Dowager Cixi (1835–1908) controlled the Qing dynasty for nearly half a century. Look at her fingernails.

Emperor Guangxu (1871–1908) of the Qing dynasty, failed to modernize China. He was poisoned by the Empress Dowager and died.
